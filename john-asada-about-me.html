Communication & Visual Design

Hi, I'm John Asada.

I design brands and build the strategy that gets them seen.

I'm a Communication student at BYU–Pathway's Ensign College (expecting to graduate in 2027), based in Nigeria, and I live at the intersection of two things people usually treat as separate: brand design and audience growth. I got into this work the practical way — building social pages and visuals because a project needed them, then getting curious about why some content connects and some just sits there. That curiosity turned into a real focus on strategic communication, social media marketing, and graphic design, all pointed at the same goal: helping a brand look like itself and get in front of the right people.

What I'm building toward is simple — I want to be the person a brand trusts with both halves of the problem: making it look right, and making sure it's actually seen. That means I'm as comfortable inside a content calendar and a growth report as I am inside a layout grid or a color palette. I bring a rich cultural grounding from Nigeria into my visual storytelling, drawing heavy inspiration from West African textiles, bold color palettes, and community-centered narratives to make graphics feel authentically vibrant and human.
