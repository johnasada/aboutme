Flag Court (Recreation)

A recreation of a professional stock photograph of a flag court — shot from a low angle to emphasize the height and repetition of the poles, the same technique the original photographer used. Shooting in a real, active public space (rather than a controlled plaza) meant working with a busier foreground than the original, which turned into the most useful lesson from the exercise: a clean foreground is as much a deliberate choice as the subject itself.
