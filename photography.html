30,000 Feet

Shot on my phone through an airplane window on a flight from Uyo to Abuja]. I built this as a poster around the photo's natural composition — the window frame's dark curve at the bottom created a built-in space for text, so I didn't need to add a heavy overlay to keep the headline readable against the sky.
